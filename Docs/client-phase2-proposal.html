Phase 2 — What We Are Proposing

Extending Your Sales Pipeline

Phase 1 covers Enquiry → Quotation. Phase 2 takes the pipeline further — all the way from sale to delivery. This means your team can manage the entire customer journey in one place.

Enquiry → Quotation → Sale → Invoice → Machine Booked → Delivery
Key benefit: No more switching between spreadsheets and the CRM. The entire sales process — from first enquiry to final delivery — lives in one system.
📝

Phase 2 — Proposed Features

💰
Sales Invoice Generation

When a quotation converts to a sale, the system generates a proper sales invoice automatically. No manual re-entry of data.

📦
Inventory Management

Track every machine in your warehouse — serial numbers, models, and availability status. Know what is in stock at a glance.

📋
Machine Booking & Delivery

When a sale is confirmed, a specific machine gets booked to that customer with a delivery date. Track who gets what and when.

👥
Multiple User Roles

Different access levels for warehouse managers and owners. Each person sees and does only what they need to.

How Inventory Connects to the CRM

The inventory system is not a standalone module. It is directly linked to your existing CRM pipeline. When a sale happens through the CRM, the inventory updates automatically — no separate data entry required.

How it works: Enquiry → Quotation → Sale confirmed → Machine from warehouse gets booked to customer → Delivery scheduled & tracked. All in one flow.
