Overview

What this platform does and who it is for.

The Unique Sorter CRM is a web-based platform that helps the team at Unique Sorter And Equipment Pvt. Ltd. manage their entire sales workflow in one place. Field engineers on site collect customer requirements and enter them into the system. The back-office team then reviews enquiries, generates quotations, and shares professional PDFs with clients.

Enquiry Management
Field engineers capture customer requirements on site through a structured form. Track each enquiry with full details, items, pricing, and follow-up dates.
Quotation Generation
Convert enquiries into professional, GST-compliant PDF quotations with one click. Share via WhatsApp or email instantly.
Team Collaboration
All team members can view enquiries, edit details, and generate quotations. Admins get additional controls.
Secure Access
Phone OTP + Password login with admin approval. Per-user screen access control ensures data security.

Registration

How to request access to the CRM.

1
Go to the Registration Page
Click "Register here" from the login page, or navigate directly to /register.
2

Enter your full name, phone number (10 digits, starting with 6–9), choose your role (User or Admin), and set a password.

Immediate vs Future Requirement

Two different modes based on the customer's timeline.

Yes — Immediate Requirement
The customer wants to buy now:
  • Items — model, size, quantity, price
  • Can add multiple items
  • Prices auto-calculate
  • Can generate a quotation
No — Future Requirement
Customer is interested but not ready:
  • Commodity — Rice, Pulses, etc.
  • Notes for follow-up
  • Next follow-up date
  • Probable month of order
  • % Chances of order (dropdown)

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and quick answers.

I'm not receiving the OTP. What should I do?

Wait for the full 60-second timer, then click "Resend OTP". Ensure your phone has SMS reception. You can also try Password login instead.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login page, switch to the Password tab and click "Forgot Password?". Enter your phone, verify with OTP, then set a new password.

I registered but can't login. Why?

New registrations require admin approval. Your account is in "pending" status until an admin approves it from Settings.

Can I login with my email address?

Yes. On the Password tab, enter your email along with your password. Your email must be stored in your account (set by admin).

Can customers see the dashboard?

No. Every page except the homepage, login, and registration requires authentication.

Can I edit a quotation after saving it?

Yes. Open the quotation and modify any field. Re-download or re-share the updated PDF.

How are prices calculated?

Prices are auto-filled based on state, model, and size (chutes). They can be manually overridden in the quotation editor.

How do I delete multiple enquiries at once?

Admins can use bulk select. Check the checkboxes, then click Delete in the floating action bar.

Is the platform mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every page is fully responsive with touch-friendly forms and a slide-out sidebar on phones.
